Who We Are

C&D Pet Store is a newly opened pet shop created with love and passion for animals.

Although our shop has just opened today, we already have valuable experience in caring for and understanding pets.

Our goal is to provide pet owners with quality products, helpful advice, and friendly service. We believe that pets are more than just animals — they are part of the family.
